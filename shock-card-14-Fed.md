## Against the textbook
- **Did markets behave the way theory predicts?** [Yes. When the rates stayed higher for longer, the valuation of most companies dropped, which led to lower stock prices. Long-term bond yields rose. Oil fell after the first day, so the March move was about rates, not oil.]
- **What surprised you?** [Gold prices went down, not up. Usually, during geopolitical unrests, the price of precious metals go up. Because gold pays no interest, rising real Treasury yields increase the opportunity cost of holding it, causing rate-hike/hold fears to overpower safe-haven buying.]
