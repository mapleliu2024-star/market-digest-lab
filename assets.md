S&P 500 (^GSPC): the broad US stock market; the major indicator of most events

WTI crude oil (CL=F): the benchmark price of US oil; the price geopolitics hits first

10Y US Treasury Yield (^TNX): the interest rate US government pays to borrow for 10 years; indicator of the economy's inflation pressure

DXY (DX-Y.NYB): the US dollar strength compared to other currencies; rises when market panics

Gold (GC=F): safe-haven asset; investors buy in Gold when feeling panic

VIX (^VIX): market's sentiment level; VIX reflects investors' expectations

XLK (XLK): Technology Select Sector SPDR ETF; provides exposure to US technology leaders within the S&P 500 index; catches AI/growth shocks

XLE (XLE): Energy Select Sector SPDR ETF; tracks the performance of the energy sector of the S&P 500; catches energy shocks

XLF (XLF): Financial Select Sector SPDR Fund; provides exposure to large-cap US financial companies in the S&P 500; catches banks' move with interest rates

Windows:
1-day = the knee-jerk reaction
1-week = did the move stick or fade
1-month =  did it change the trend
