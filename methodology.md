(1) What counts as an event? 
Central bank decisions that surprises the market; geopolitical shocks; tariff or trade announcements; major economic data surprises such as job reports; big product launches that moved a sector.

(2) What does not count as an event?
Ordinary daily wiggles; rumors without a clear timestamp; stories that I cannot define or verify.

(3) What must every event have?
Exact date; source link; I can explain it in one-line "why it is a shock".

Note: for the overlap events, if two candidate events fail in the same window, pick the dominant one, or note the overlap.
